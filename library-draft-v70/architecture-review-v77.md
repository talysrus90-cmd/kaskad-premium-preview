# V76 / V77 architecture review

V76 implements five core V75 briefs as original, independent exterior geometry. V77 improves camera fit, glass tone and garden detail. Existing V69 site remains untouched. Area is schematic gross heated area, not net or cadastral area; attic allowance is 0.65. No external copyrighted plans or renders imported.

## Review observations
- K75-01: long gable form, asymmetric timber entrance and grouped living-room glazing; gable timber still needs proper photographed cladding and eave detailing.
- K75-02: main and subsidiary gables, timber/masonry entry; inspect adjoining roof flashing and structural junction before approval.
- K75-03: L-shaped flat-roof garden house; needs real recessed entrance, roof drainage and facade module review. Small pier beside entry needs redesign and structural sizing.
- K75-04: steep roof and central attic window; no dormer or attic plan yet. Camera fit added in V77.
- K75-05: two-storey main volume and lower garden wing; needs better facade articulation and actual room layout.
